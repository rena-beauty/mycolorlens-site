Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

MyColorLens (the "App") respects user privacy and handles information according to the policy below.

1. Information the App Handles

(1) Information stored only on your device
The brand name, color name, and diameter of lenses you register, along with photos and reviews, are stored only on your device and are never sent to any external server or to the operator. If iCloud Backup is enabled on your device, this data may be included in your own iCloud backup.

(2) Information sent and published as part of the shared catalog
When you use "Add to Catalog," the brand name, product name, diameter, color group, and barcode number (if entered) are sent to an external database (Google Firebase) and published to all users of the App. This does not include personal information such as your name or contact details.

(3) Information sent via the Contact form
If you contact us through the "Contact Us" form, the email address (optional), category, subject, and message you enter are sent to an external database (Google Firebase). This is used only to respond to your inquiry and is never shared with other users.

(4) Camera access
We may request camera access for the barcode scanning feature. Camera footage is used only for on-screen scanning and is never saved or transmitted.

2. Purpose of Use
Information handled by the App is used only for the following purposes:
- To let you record and manage your lenses
- To provide in-app features (trend analysis, catalog search, etc.)
- To share the shared catalog with other users of the App
- To respond to your inquiries
- To process Apple billing for the Premium Plan (billing itself is processed entirely within Apple's systems; the App never collects payment details such as credit card numbers)

3. Disclosure to Third Parties
Except as required by law, the operator will not disclose collected information to third parties without consent. Shared catalog information (1-(2) above) is, by design, intended to be shared among users as part of the App's functionality. Information collected by Apple during billing is managed under Apple's own privacy policy.

4. Security
The App takes appropriate technical measures to protect the information it handles, including encrypted communication (HTTPS) and conditions on what can be written to the shared database. However, complete security cannot be guaranteed for communication over the internet. For information stored on your device (photos, reviews, etc.), please also consider your device's own security settings (passcode, etc.).

5. Use of External Services
The App uses Google's "Firebase" to power the shared catalog and contact form. Google's Privacy Policy also applies to the use of Firebase (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

6. About Advertising
If you are not subscribed to the Premium Plan, ads may be displayed within the App. Ad delivery may involve the use of identifiers and similar information by advertising providers.

7. Use by Minors
The App contains information related to a medical device (colored contact lenses). If a minor uses the App, please do so with a guardian's consent. The App does not directly collect personal information, but if a minor enters an email address in the Contact form, please have a guardian confirm this beforehand.

8. Deleting Your Data
Data stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the App. If you would like information you submitted to the shared catalog or contact form to be deleted, please contact us via "Contact Us" in Settings.

9. Changes to This Policy
This policy may be changed without notice due to changes in law or in the App's services. Revised policies take effect once displayed within the App, so please check back periodically.
